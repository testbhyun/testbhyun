# Popular Bread Types Around the World 🥖

## European Breads

### Baguette
A long, thin French bread with a crispy crust and airy interior. It's perfect for sandwiches and is a staple in French cuisine.

### Sourdough
A tangy bread made with a fermented starter culture. It has a distinctive sour flavor and chewy texture, with a crispy crust.

### Focaccia
An Italian flatbread topped with olive oil, rosemary, and salt. It's soft and fluffy inside with a golden, dimpled surface.

### Ciabatta
An Italian bread known for its irregular shape and holes. It has a crispy crust and a light, airy crumb.

## American Breads

### Whole Wheat
Made from whole grain flour, this bread is nutrient-dense and has a nutty flavor. It's denser and darker than white bread.

### Cornbread
A sweet bread made with cornmeal, popular in Southern cuisine. It has a slightly crumbly texture and can be made with or without sugar.

### Rye Bread
A dense bread with a rich, earthy flavor. It's common in Eastern European and Scandinavian cuisines.

## Asian Breads

### Naan
A soft, fluffy Indian flatbread traditionally cooked in a tandoor. It's often brushed with butter and garlic.

### Mantou
A Chinese steamed bun made with wheat flour dough. It's soft, pillowy, and slightly sweet.

## Quick Breads

### Banana Bread
A sweet bread made with ripe bananas. It's moist, flavorful, and often studded with nuts or chocolate chips.

### Irish Soda Bread
A dense bread leavened with baking soda instead of yeast. It has a slightly sweet flavor and is traditionally made with buttermilk.
